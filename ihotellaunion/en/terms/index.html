01

Bookings and payments

An inquiry sent through WhatsApp does not constitute a confirmed booking. The room, rate, dates, payment method and any cancellation conditions are confirmed only when Hotel La Unión expressly confirms them in writing.

Prices may vary by season, occupancy and room type. Before making a payment, verify that the details and account information were sent through an official hotel channel.

02

Stay conditions

Check-in and check-out times, room capacity and house rules will be provided when your booking is confirmed. Guests are responsible for providing accurate information about the number of adults and children.

To protect everyone's comfort and because of the characteristics of our facilities, we currently do not accept pets. If you have a special requirement, please ask before booking.

03

Tours, transportation and activities

Some services are provided directly and others by partner operators. Availability, routes, schedules, inclusions and rates are subject to confirmation. Published prices are references from the 2026 price list and may change.

Weather, road conditions, borders, or decisions by authorities and operators may alter a departure. When this happens, we will help review alternatives according to the provider's applicable policies.

04

Privacy and reservation form

The form saves dates, guest counts and your transfer preference in this browser for 24 hours after your last change. Use “Clear selection” to delete them, especially on a shared device. Expired selections are discarded when you open the form and removed while it remains open. When you continue through WhatsApp, the information is shared directly with Hotel La Unión through that service and is also subject to WhatsApp's policies.

We do not process payments or store card details on this website.

05

Is anything unclear?

Message us before confirming. We would rather answer a question in time.
